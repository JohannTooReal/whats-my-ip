### How IP Detection Works

In order to determine your public IP address, the website communicates with the **ipify API** through a secure external network request. This request:

* Originates directly from your device
* Travels across the internet to identify your public-facing IP
* Is processed by the ipify service (third-party provider)

### Important Privacy Notes

The website itself does **not** collect, retain, or transmit your IP address to a privately operated database or storage system. The displayed IP address is obtained solely for the purpose of providing you with immediate feedback on your current public IP.

However, it is important to understand that:

* Any online service used to determine a public IP address may have access to the IP address from which the request originates
* Your public IP address is visible to any web server you connect to (this is how internet routing works)
* For complete anonymity online, consider using a VPN or proxy service
* Your Internet Service Provider (ISP) always has access to your IP address information

## 🛠️ How It Works

### Technical Overview

The website uses the **[ipify API](https://api.ipify.org/)** to determine your public IP address. Here's the process:

1. **Page Load** - When you visit the website, JavaScript automatically initiates a request
2. **API Request** - A secure HTTPS request is sent to the ipify API
3. **IP Detection** - The ipify service identifies your public IP address
4. **Display** - Your public IP address is displayed directly on the webpage in real-time

### Why ipify?

* ✅ Fast and reliable API
* ✅ Free service with no rate limiting for basic usage
* ✅ Works with IPv4 and IPv6 addresses
* ✅ No API key required for basic functionality
* ✅ HTTPS encryption for secure communication

## ℹ️ Important Notes

### Public IP vs. Private IP

This website displays your **public IP address** (also called external IP):

* **Public IP Example**: `203.0.113.45`
* **What it is**: The IP address visible to the entire internet
* **What you use it for**: Web browsing, email, video calls, online gaming

This is **different** from your **private/local IP address** (also called internal IP):

* **Private IP Example**: `192.168.1.1` or `10.0.0.5`
* **What it is**: The IP address on your local home or office network
* **What you use it for**: Connecting to devices on your network (printer, router, etc.)

### What You Can Learn From Your IP

Your public IP address can reveal:

* 📍 Approximate geographical location (city/region level)
* 🌍 Internet Service Provider (ISP)
* 🏢 General organization or company (if using corporate network)

### What Your IP Cannot Reveal

Your public IP address **cannot** directly reveal:

* ❌ Your exact street address (only approximate location)
* ❌ Your name or personal identity
* ❌ Your browsing history
* ❌ Your passwords or personal files
* ❌ Your private network devices

## 📚 Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is this website safe to use?
**A**: Yes! The website is completely safe. It does not collect personal information, use cookies, or install anything on your device.

### Q: Why does my IP change sometimes?
**A**: Your ISP may assign you a new IP address periodically, or you may be connecting from a different network (home, work, mobile, etc.).

### Q: Can I hide my public IP?
**A**: You can mask your public IP by using a VPN (Virtual Private Network) service. When connected to a VPN, websites will see the VPN's IP instead of your real IP.

### Q: What is IPv6?
**A**: IPv6 is the newer internet protocol that provides a much larger pool of IP addresses. Many networks now support both IPv4 and IPv6.

### Q: Who owns my IP address?
**A**: Your ISP owns and manages your IP address. They assign it to you for your internet connection.

### Q: Is my IP address permanent?
**A**: Most residential ISPs use dynamic IP addresses, which may change. Business/enterprise IPs are often static (permanent).
